COMMUNITIES · YOUTH · LOCAL KNOWLEDGE

The objective

Local leadership.
Shared progress.

Communities hold essential knowledge about their environments and the changes affecting them. Ecosynic puts this experience at the centre of climate and environmental action.

We support inclusive participation and meaningful roles for women and young people in learning, local decision-making and the development of practical solutions.
