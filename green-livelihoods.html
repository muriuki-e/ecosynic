The objective

Opportunity that

A climate-resilient future also depends on sustainable economic opportunity. Ecosynic supports approaches that connect enterprise, climate action and practical skills.

We focus on green businesses and circular solutions that can contribute to stronger local economies, with particular attention to youth employment and women-led enterprise.
