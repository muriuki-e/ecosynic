The objective

Restore nature.
Strengthen stewardship.

Healthy ecosystems support biodiversity, water resources, livelihoods and climate resilience. Ecosynic focuses on restoring degraded landscapes while supporting the people who care for them.

Our approach brings community stewardship together with locally adapted restoration knowledge, so efforts respond to the character and needs of each landscape.
