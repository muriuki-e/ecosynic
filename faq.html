QUESTIONS & ANSWERS

What would you
like to know?

Which counties are included in Ecosynic’s regional focus?

The current regional focus includes Meru, Isiolo, Embu and Tharaka-Nithi. See the geographic scope page. These counties describe the broader focus area, not a verified list of active project sites.

How does Ecosynic report its impact?

The impact section presents reach figures as minimum thresholds and notes that indicators will be updated as results are verified. For program-specific evidence or accountability information, contact the team by email.

Can I donate online or make an in-kind contribution?

This website does not accept online payments. To discuss a potential financial or in-kind contribution and confirm the appropriate next steps, visit Get involved or email info@ecosynic.or.ke. Do not send payment or banking details by email.

Are volunteer, internship, job or tender applications open?

Applications and tender submissions are handled by email enquiry only; this site does not have an application portal. Email info@ecosynic.or.ke to ask about current opportunities. See volunteering, careers and tenders.

How can I receive Ecosynic updates?

Subscribe on the newsletter page. Your email is stored securely for updates, and each newsletter includes an unsubscribe link.
